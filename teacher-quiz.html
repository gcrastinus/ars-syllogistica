How to use

Select one or more exercise sets from the course of study, choose the level and how many questions you want from each, then generate. Every question is freshly drawn from the same generators the students use. The answer key prints on its own page(s) at the end — separate those pages before handing the quiz out. Use your browser’s Print → Save as PDF.

Study sections (Grammar, Orientation, Diagram Workshop, Square of Opposition) are not included here.
