## 2. NEW MATERIAL (11 min) — a short telling, then the One-Over-Many check

### 2A. Retelling the opening of the Meno (narration with choral checks; not a memorized dialogue)

**MODEL**

**Virtue means goodness of character — being brave, just, wise. Keep that word. We need it for the story.**

**A young man named Meno asked Socrates: can virtue be taught?**

**Socrates said: I cannot know whether virtue can be taught, because I do not know what virtue is.**

**When I do not know the "what" of anything, how can I know its properties?**

**Meno then gave many answers. A man's virtue. A woman's virtue. A child's virtue. One for every age and condition.**

**Socrates said that was a swarm, like a swarm of bees.**

**Bees differ in size and beauty. But do bees differ as bees? No. There is one nature by which all bees are bees.**

**Likewise there must be one nature by which all virtues are virtue. The answer must fix its eye on that.**

**So the opening of the Meno is three sentences.**

(Write them, numbered, and leave them up:)

```
1. Meno asked whether virtue can be taught.
2. Socrates said he cannot know that until he knows what virtue is.
3. Meno gave a swarm of virtues; Socrates said there is one nature by which all bees are bees — and one nature by which all virtues are virtue.
```

**LEAD** (teacher joins, then fades)

→ whether virtue can be taught

### 2B. The One-Over-Many check: ANSWER or SWARM

**MODEL**

**Here is the lesson we will keep. An answer to "What is X?" must be one answer that fits every X.**

**When someone answers "What is X?" with a list of X's, say: That is a swarm, not an answer.**

(Write and leave up:)

```
ANSWER = one answer that fits every X
SWARM  = a list of X's
```

**I will ask "What is a bird?" and give a reply. You say ANSWER or SWARM.**

**A wrong one-line answer is still an ANSWER. A list of birds is a SWARM. We are not grading the answer yet. We are only asking: is it one answer, or a list?**

**What is a bird? A bird is an animal with feathers.** (pause) **That is one answer. ANSWER.**

**What is a bird? Robins, crows, and ducks.** (pause) **That is a list of birds. SWARM.**

**Listen to those two again. A bird is an animal with feathers.** (pause) **ANSWER. Robins, crows, and ducks.** (pause) **SWARM.**

**What is a dog? A dog is an animal that barks.** (pause) **ANSWER. Even if that answer is not perfect, it is one answer.**

**What is a dog? Collies, beagles, and labs.** (pause) **SWARM.**

**What is a tool? A tool is a thing we make to work better.** (pause) **ANSWER.**

**What is a tool? Hammers, saws, and wrenches.** (pause) **SWARM.**

#### CORRECTION — 2B

## 9. DRAFTER'S FLAGS

1. **"Virtue" is not a working word for every 9-year-old.** Fix already applied: one plain gloss at the start of 2A ("goodness of character — being brave, just, wise") and then the story uses the word. No extra moral lecture.

2. **A weak one-line definition will be called SWARM because it is "wrong."** This is the most likely 9-year-old error. Fix already applied: the model says outright that a wrong one-liner is still ANSWER; the first dog pair uses a slightly imperfect answer ("an animal that barks") on purpose; the correction names that exact mistake.

3. **A list of real X's will be called ANSWER because each item is truly an X.** Fix already applied: the correction says "that is why it is a swarm of birds, not an answer," and every negative is a bare list of instances, not a list of properties.

4. **A list of properties ("a bird has wings, feathers, and a beak") is ambiguous** (one subject, several marks — not a swarm of birds). Those items are not in the sequence. Spare items stay with instance-lists only.

5. **The Meno retell could balloon.** Fix already applied: three numbered sentences on the board; any extra plot (slave boy, recollection) is cut with "Not yet." The slave-boy scene is reserved for Lesson 3 (preview) and Lesson 28 (full).

6. **Deeper connects act one to definition.** That is the brief's own extension, not new doctrine for both. The word *definition* is planted for Lesson 3; it is not drilled with the 9-year-old today.

7. **Review is interleaved, not blocked.** Act-sorts, wordings, and the new ANSWER/SWARM items share the guided block so yesterday's track does not sit in a clump and go cold.
